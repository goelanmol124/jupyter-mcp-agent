Biexponential fits are only locally identifiable from a single curve: swapping (A1,k1) with (A2,k2) gives the same function if both exponentials appear. Very bad starts can hit bounds, negative amplitudes, or covariance blow-ups; in practice, multiple random restarts or bounded parameters help.
